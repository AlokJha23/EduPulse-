# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in the Academiq Education Platform community and institutional deployments a respectful, harassment-free, and inclusive experience for educators, students, administrators, and contributors.

## Expected Behavior
- Use welcoming, respectful, and constructive language in all communications.
- Respect student and institutional privacy at all times.
- Accept constructive technical feedback gracefully and focus on what is best for learners and educators.

## Unacceptable Behavior
- Harassment, discrimination, or abusive behavior of any kind.
- Unauthorized sharing of private student information, credentials, or institutional records.
- Deliberate attempts to disrupt classroom operations, assessments, or platform security.

## Enforcement
Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainer at **alokkumar02012@gmail.com** and will be reviewed promptly and confidentially.
